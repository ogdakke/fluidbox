@ogdakke/fluidbox

A lightbox that moves like the page is physical.

Media flies out of its thumbnail, follows your fingers, and lands back where it came from. One runtime for custom elements, plain JavaScript, React, Solid, Vue and Angular.

01  what you get

Motion that keeps its place

Shared-element flights

Opening and closing fly between thumbnail and viewer, crop and corners included.

Gestures that hand off

Native swiping with momentum, pinch and wheel zoom, drag down to dismiss.

Lazy item lists

Describe a million items by index. You render only the thumbnails you show.

Any media

Images, video, iframes and your own components share one slide strip.

Filmstrip and sheet

Optional add-ons: a thumbnail strip, and a panel with snap points.

Headless when you need it

Bring your own controls and captions; the runtime keeps the motion.

02  your stack

The same gallery, six ways

Every binding drives the same runtime and supports the same patterns. Pick yours; the docs remember it.
