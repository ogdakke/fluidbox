# Damaged Helmet

`damaged-helmet.glb` is the **Damaged Helmet** sample model from the Khronos Group's
[glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/DamagedHelmet),
unchanged. `damaged-helmet.webp` and `damaged-helmet-thumb.webp` are stills rendered from it with
`<model-viewer>`, so they are covered by the same terms.
