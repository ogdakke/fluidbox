Which layers tint the toolbar?

iOS 26 Safari colours its status bar and toolbar from fixed or sticky elements at the viewport's edges. Open this page on an iPhone, scroll down so the toolbar shrinks, then try each probe and the lightbox under each setting. Note whether the bars show the layer behind them (see-through) or turn a solid colour (tinted).

1 · Probes

A magenta layer, built four ways. Tap the layer to remove it. Nothing else on the page is fixed or sticky unless you turn the header on below.

2 · Fluidbox settings

Each change reloads the page with the setting in the URL.

3 · Gallery

Tap a photo. Pinch or double-tap to zoom, which adds the zoom viewport. Swipe down to dismiss.
