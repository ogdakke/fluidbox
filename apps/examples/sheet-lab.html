Sheet lab

Scroll to dismiss, scroll for info

A prototype in plain HTML and CSS: every slide is a vertical scroll-snap sheet with three resting points. Pull the photo down to dismiss it, push it up to reveal the info panel. The browser's own scroll physics move it, and scroll-driven animations fade the backdrop and fit the photo, so none of it waits on JavaScript.

1 · Settings

Each change reloads the page with the setting in the URL.

2 · Gallery

Tap a photo. Swipe sideways between photos, down to dismiss, up for the info panel. On a desktop, scroll with a trackpad or press I.
