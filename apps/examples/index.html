@ogdakke/fluidbox

A lightbox that moves like the page is physical.

Images fly out of their thumbnails and land back where they came from. One runtime, shared by plain custom elements, a headless controller, and every framework binding.

01  gallery

Plain markup, any aspect ratio

Portrait, square, and panorama images keep their shape in flight. Open one and swipe through the rest.

Markup 
<script type="module">
  import "@ogdakke/fluidbox/elements";
  import "@ogdakke/fluidbox/styles.css";
</script>

<fluidbox-gallery aria-label="Landscapes">
  <fluidbox-item item-id="dune">
    <button slot="trigger" type="button" data-fluidbox-preview>
      <img src="/art/dune.svg" alt="Dune at golden hour" width="1800" height="1200" />
    </button>
    <template slot="content">
      <img src="/art/dune.svg" alt="Dune at golden hour" width="1800" height="1200" />
    </template>
    <span slot="caption">Dune at golden hour</span>
  </fluidbox-item>
  <!-- …more items -->
  <fluidbox-filmstrip></fluidbox-filmstrip>
</fluidbox-gallery>

02  integrations

Pick how you build

Every page runs the same runtime. Pick the one that matches your stack.

01

Elements →

Declarative <fluidbox-gallery> markup, plus an indexed 100,000-item source.

02

Headless →

createFluidbox() with your own thumbnails and a portal for custom chrome.

03

React →

Composed parts, rich origins, a live iframe, and a virtualized photo library.

 04

Solid →

useFluidboxGallery with source switching and an indexed window.

 05

Vue →

<FluidboxGallery> with source switching and an indexed window.

06

Angular →

Signals and useFluidboxGallery, zoneless, with an indexed window.
